02 What it checks

Three signals, one verdict

No single clue proves a review is fake. All three are read together before anything is flagged.

Signal 01

Sentiment analysis

Each review runs through VADER, which returns a compound score between −1 and +1 along with a label. The score is saved on the review, so products can later be ordered by how their reviews actually read.

Signal 02

Duplicate detection

Copy-pasted praise is the cheapest way to move a rating. Matching text from the same user on the same product is marked as a duplicate.

Signal 03

IP tracking

The address behind each submission is recorded, so a run of glowing reviews arriving from one place is visible rather than hidden.

Result

Ranked by what survives

Products are ordered by the average sentiment of the reviews that pass the checks — not by how many reviews they collected.

03 How it works

From submission to verdict

The whole pipeline runs the moment a review is posted. Nothing waits in a queue.

1

Review submitted

A signed-in user writes a review on a product. The text and the originating IP address are captured together.

2

Analysed automatically

VADER produces a sentiment score and label, while the text is compared against what that user already wrote for the product.

3

Flagged or approved

Suspicious entries are marked as fake, and every product is ranked by the average sentiment of the reviews that survive.
